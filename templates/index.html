How Resolution Works

1

Report the Issue

Students submit problems specifying location, category, urgency priority, and optional photos.

→
2

Admin Review & Assign

College administrators review incoming complaints and dispatch them to the relevant staff or department.

→
3

Staff Action & Fix

Maintenance staff investigate, post status updates, and mark the complaint resolved once completed.

→
4

Student Confirmation

Students receive in-app notifications and can close the ticket or reopen if the fix needs attention.
